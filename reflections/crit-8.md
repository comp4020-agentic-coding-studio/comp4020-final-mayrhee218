# Crit 8 — It's alive!

**What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?**

The breakthrough wasn't technical, it was in scope. I started with a TikTok-scale
pitch — anyone, anywhere, making virtual-human videos — and it wasn't until I
actually sat down to define what "good" meant that the project got buildable.
Reading about home-cooked apps forced the question of who this is actually
for, and the honest answer was "a few specific people," not "anyone." Once I
reframed the core loop as presence and traces instead of views and followers,
the first version stopped being an impossible AR/3D/motion-capture system and
became a small shared page: pick an avatar, leave a trace, find someone
else's trace later. That's what let something real ship this week instead of
another week of scoping the whole vision.

**What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?**

I want to get better at deciding what a system is *for* before deciding what
it's *built with*. It was tempting to jump straight to the AR/3D stack because
that's the exciting part of the idea, but the thing that actually needed
answering first was much smaller: what does one real visitor do here, and why
would they come back? I also noticed how different it feels to direct an
agent through that scoping conversation versus writing every line myself —
the value I added was in the judgment calls (what to cut, what counts as
"alive"), not in the code, and I want to get more deliberate about using that
time well.
